dotfiles.io uses the [Lucid](https://themes.static-site-generator.com/lucid/) theme for [SSG](https://static-site-generator.com/), the same theme as the documentation at [doc.dotfiles.io](https://doc.dotfiles.io/). Its colour tokens are held to WCAG AAA contrast (7:1 for text) in both light and dark schemes.

Pages are static HTML. Scripts only add the theme button and the collapsing menu on small screens. The site follows your system's light or dark preference unless you choose one with the theme button, and respects reduced-motion settings.

If something is hard to use, please [open an issue](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/dotfiles.github.io/issues) describing the page and what went wrong.
