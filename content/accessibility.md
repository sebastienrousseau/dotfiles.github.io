dotfiles.io uses the [Voxt](https://themes.static-site-generator.com/voxt/) theme for [SSG](https://static-site-generator.com/), whose colour tokens are held to WCAG AAA contrast (7:1 for text) in both light and dark schemes.

The site works without JavaScript, follows your system's light or dark preference unless you choose one with the theme button, and respects reduced-motion settings.

If something is hard to use, please [open an issue](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/dotfiles.github.io/issues) describing the page and what went wrong.
